## Position-Based Training Paths

### Point Guard (PG) — "The Architect"

**Focus:** Ball handling, court vision, pull-up shooting, leadership
**Week 1–2 (Rookie):** Stationary dribbling, form shooting, defensive stance, basic pivots
**Week 3–4 (Rookie→Varsity):** Cone zig-zag, 5-spot shooting, zig-zag slides
**Week 5–8 (Varsity):** Combo moves, off-dribble pull-ups, transition pull-ups, pick-and-roll reads
**Week 9–12 (Elite):** Speed dribble finishes, game-speed shooting, full-court press positioning

### Shooting Guard (SG) — "The Bucket Getter"

**Focus:** Shooting off movement, creating off dribble, athleticism
**Week 1–2:** Form shooting, figure-8 dribble, box jumps
**Week 3–4:** Curl cuts, corner 3s, lateral bounds
**Week 5–8:** Off-screen curls, step-back jumper, depth drops
**Week 9–12:** Step-back 3, game-speed 5-spot, approach verticals

### Small Forward (SF) — "The Swiss Army Knife"

**Focus:** Versatility, mid-range, help defense, athleticism
**Week 1–2:** Layups, defensive stance, broad jumps
**Week 3–4:** Catch-and-shoot, closeout + contest, sprint + backpedal
**Week 5–8:** Off-dribble pull-ups, help-and-recover, agility ladder
**Week 9–12:** Floater series, pick-and-roll defense, 17s

### Power Forward (PF) — "The Enforcer"

**Focus:** Post footwork, mid-range, rebounding positioning, physicality
**Week 1–2:** Bank shots, jab step series, box jumps
**Week 3–4:** Pivot footwork, mikan drill, lateral bounds
**Week 5–8:** Step-back jumper, closeout drills, depth drops
**Week 9–12:** Floater series, 1-on-1 no-help defense, approach verticals

### Center (C) — "The Anchor"

**Focus:** Post moves, finishing, shot blocking positioning, conditioning
**Week 1–2:** Form shooting (close), pivot footwork, defensive stance
**Week 3–4:** Mikan drill, charge taking, box jumps
**Week 5–8:** Step-back mid-range, shell drill, sprint + backpedal
**Week 9–12:** Two-ball series, pick-and-roll defense, 17s
